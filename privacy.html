Wonderpass Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Wonderpass mobile application, called Kâşif in Turkish, and its related services (the "App") handle information. The App is a learning game for children, set up and managed by a parent. It is operated by NouSphere Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("NouSphere Labs", "we", "us" or "our"). If you have questions, contact us at info@nouspherelabs.com.

In short
  • There is no account. We do not ask for an email address, phone number or photos.
  • The child's explorer is put together on the device from ready-made parts. No photo of the child is taken, chosen or sent anywhere.
  • Everything the App keeps (the explorer's first name or nickname, age, picture, progress and personal stories) stays on the device.
  • Only when a Plus subscriber makes a personal story is limited information sent to our AI partners, Perplexity and fal.ai, to write, draw and narrate it (see section 2).
  • The App has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share personal information.
  • Settings, purchases and links out of the App are behind a parental gate.

1. Information kept on the device

During setup, a parent enters the explorer's first name or a nickname and the child's age, and builds the explorer's look (figure, skin tone, hair style and colour). The age is used only to make games easier or harder. This information, the child's stars and progress, and any personal stories are stored only on the device. We do not have a copy.

You can change the explorer or age in Settings. Deleting the App removes all of this information.

2. Personal stories (Plus)

Plus subscribers can ask for a personal story in which the explorer is the hero. To make one, the App sends the following over an encrypted connection:

ProviderWhat is sentPurpose
Perplexity AI, Inc.The explorer's first name or nickname, the story's topic and its fact list, the child's age band and the App languageWriting the story text
fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc.)The explorer picture (a cartoon built in the App, never a photo), short scene descriptions and the story textDrawing the pictures and narrating the story with a Google text-to-speech model

No photo, contact detail, location or device identifier is sent. The finished story is saved on the device. We do not run servers that store these requests; the providers process and keep them under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not allow them to use this information for advertising. Because the name is sent, we recommend using a nickname.

3. Pictures you save

If you save a story picture, the App asks iOS for permission to add images to your photo library. It can only add pictures; it cannot see your library.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The App checks your subscription status with Apple on the device.

5. Content reports and messages

You can report a personal story from the story screen. This opens an email to us in your mail app; the story itself is not sent. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply and keep a record of your request.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not show ads or use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising.
  • We do not collect photos, voice recordings, location or contacts.
  • We do not use any information to train AI models.

7. Children

The App is made for children and is meant to be set up by a parent or legal guardian. Apart from the personal stories described in section 2, which a parent turns on by subscribing behind the parental gate, no personal information about the child leaves the device. A parent can contact us at any time with questions about the child's information. We follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and comparable laws where they apply.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Turkey or a U.S. state with a privacy law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the App keeps information on the device, you can see, change or delete most of it yourself. For anything else, email info@nouspherelabs.com. Where personal stories are made, we rely on your consent and on providing the subscription you asked for.

9. Users outside the United States

We are based in the United States, and our AI partners may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

10. Security

All requests are sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and, for material changes, give notice in the App.
